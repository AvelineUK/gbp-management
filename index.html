What Is a Google Business Profile?

When someone searches for a service like "emergency electrician near me" or "plumber in Bristol," Google shows a list of local businesses before the regular search results. That list? Those are Google Business Profiles.

Your Google Business Profile is the information box that appears when people search for your business or your services. It shows your:

  • Business name and location
  • Opening hours and phone number
  • Customer reviews and ratings
  • Photos of your work
  • Services you offer



For local businesses, your Google Business Profile is more important than your website. It's often the first thing potential customers see, and it directly influences whether they call you or your competitor. While having a Google Business Profile is free and easy, keeping it optimized, active, and professional takes consistent time and effort most business owners simply don't have.

How It Works

Here's exactly what happens when you sign up, and what you can expect ongoing.

1

Initial Audit

I go through your entire Google Business Profile, looking for areas that can be improved. This includes checking your business information, photos, services, reviews, and how you compare to competitors in your area.

Why this matters: Most profiles have issues you don't even know exist—wrong categories, missing information, or inconsistent details across different platforms. These small problems can significantly impact whether customers find you.

2

Directory Check

I review your business listings on other websites and directories to ensure all your information is consistent everywhere.

Why this matters: Google cross-references your information across the web. If your phone number is different on Yell than it is on Google, it creates confusion and can hurt your rankings.

3

Profile Optimization

I fix errors, update outdated information, add missing services, and ensure your profile is complete and optimized for local search.

Why this matters: Google favours complete, detailed profiles. The more relevant information you provide, the more likely you are to appear in local searches.

4

Review Response Catch-Up

I reply to all unanswered reviews from the last six months, plus any unanswered negative reviews (2 stars or less) regardless of age. All responses are professional, thoughtful, and represent your business well.

Why this matters: Potential customers read your review responses. How you handle feedback—especially criticism—tells them a lot about how you'll treat them as a customer.

5

Ongoing Management

After the initial setup, I handle your profile's day-to-day management:

  • Respond to new reviews as soon as possible (usually within 72 hours)
  • Upload fresh photos regularly
  • Keep your services and information current
  • Monitor your profile for any issues
  • Adapt to new Google features as they're released

Why this matters: Google rewards active profiles. Regular updates signal that your business is operating and engaged with customers, which improves your visibility in local search.

6

Monthly Reporting

Each month, I send you a simple, one-page report showing how your Google Business Profile is performing—how many people are finding you, what actions they're taking, and how your visibility is trending.

Why this matters: The report is straightforward and takes less than two minutes to read, but gives you clear insight into your return on investment.
